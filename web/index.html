Accounts

Some sites only show their pictures to signed-in people. Sign in here and Still Heart uses your account for that site. Your password goes to the site, not to this app.

Required: the site won't work without signing in. Often needed: only a few public pages work without it. Optional: public pages work, and signing in adds private or age-restricted ones.

Download folder

Files are saved in subfolders by site and author unless you change organization below.

Other ways to sign in

If a site won't let you sign in under Accounts, sign in with your own browser and pick it here. Firefox works best, because Chrome's newer cookie encryption often blocks this. A Netscape cookies.txt from any browser also works.

About

Still Heart is a portable gallery-dl front end. Copy StillHeart.exe to another Windows PC and run it. You must have the right to save what you download.
